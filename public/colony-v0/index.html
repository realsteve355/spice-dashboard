Colony v0 — pre-AXION baseline

Mesa agent-based simulation · 100 citizens · 240 months · static snapshot · back to zpc.finance
Step 1 — Maryfontaine today. 100 adult citizens (with ~40 children as dependents) in a chain-dominated small town. Each sector has three private providers: a chain branch (Walmart / McDonald's — employs citizens at low wages, ~70% of revenue drains to corporate HQ), a truly-local indy (the independent shop, the artisan, the dentist — revenue stays local, better wages), and pure imports (Amazon, streaming — no local jobs, all revenue drains). Plus a public sector (~23% of workforce — teachers, town-hall, healthcare workers) funded by federal/state transfers. Households with kids get child-credit transfers matched to their consumption boost. The colony's BoP is calibrated to balance: chain corp fees + pure imports flow OUT; exports + federal transfers + child credits flow IN.

What's in the basket (Grok V9.13 Y20 reference)

Three consumption categories in our model (food / goods / services) map to Grok's seven-category moderate basket. Grok V9.13 Y20 family-of-4 total: $655-950 Mond/month. UBI for a family of 4 = ~$783/month → comfortable surplus after the Colony Bill (utilities/LVT/Fed line).

Note: housing (rent, mortgage interest, property tax) is modelled separately from the basket — those flows go via the Bank/Landlord entity and the public sector, not via the three consumption sectors. Federal/state income tax is also separate. The basket here is the post-tax, post-housing consumption pie.

Pension policy — three groups (MF Pensions Policy v1.0)

Existing pension promises are honoured. Pensions are paid in addition to UBI, never clawed back. The other 53% of adults not in the workforce are not all retirees — ~21% of adults are 65+ Group 1 retirees (full $400/mo pension + UBI), the other ~32% are students, full-time caregivers, and disabled (UBI only — no separate safety-net payment in this model; existing federal/state safety-net spending is folded into the public-sector transfer line).
Current Retirees (Group 1)
Full existing pension continues unchanged. Receive pension + UBI. Many comfortably better off as deflation lowers basket cost.
Mid-Career (Group 2)
Accrued benefits legally protected. Mandatory new contributions phase down over 7–10 years. Voluntary saving thereafter.
Younger Workers (Group 3)
No mandatory pension contributions. UBI is the primary retirement floor. Voluntary Axion-linked savings if desired.
