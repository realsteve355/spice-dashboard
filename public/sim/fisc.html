What the Fisc does

The Fisc is the colony treasury. It brings together three annual flows already modelled elsewhere: its income is the Market Access Charge collected from the business mix; its outgoings are UBI / welfare — one per-person basket paid to each recipient; and the gap accumulates as a balance, with reserves earning a 10-year-bond yield.

Outgoings = the per-person basket × the number of recipients. By default that's the unemployed (welfare during the transition); switch to universal to pay every adult. Use the controls to find the charge rate that keeps the Fisc solvent.

Fisc balance — cumulative reserves

The running balance: prior reserves plus each year's net, with reserves compounding at the bond yield. Below zero means the Fisc is borrowing.
