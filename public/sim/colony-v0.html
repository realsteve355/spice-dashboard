MaryFontaine Colony — The AXION Abundance Model

Turning AI-driven unemployment into shared prosperity
AI and robotics are displacing workers faster than policy can adapt. By Year 20, conventional employment serves only ~25% of working-age adults — yet the AXION model turns this collapse into a transition to abundance. A Market Access Charge (MAC) on AI/robotics corporate profits funds Universal Basic Income for every adult. Income and sales taxes go to zero; a Colony Bill of $42/mo utilities + 0.35% LVT + a small federal contribution replaces them. The local currency (Mond) keeps consumption flowing inside the colony. The result: living standards improve as employment falls; work becomes optional; tax burdens drop ~85%; UBI grows past basket cost; saved wealth accumulates. Below: the model run, the data, the proof.

03. The transition story

What this shows: four trajectories over 240 months. Unemployment rises (top-left) as automation displaces workers. UBI grows (top-right) because the MAC pool grows with corporate profits. Total consumption stays sustained (bottom-left) — the closed-loop proof: UBI replaces wages as the income source, and Mond keeps purchasing power circulating inside the colony. Citizen tax burden (bottom-right) drops sharply under AXION vs Traditional — the single most important comparison on this page.

The chart below tracks family-of-4 basket cost (deflating with automation) against family-of-4 UBI (growing with the MAC pool). UBI overtakes basket cost; food, shelter and services are reliably affordable on UBI alone.

05. Saved wealth — abundance compounds

What this shows: liquid savings inside the colony, external investments (401k / IRA / brokerage), and property equity (home value − mortgage balance). Wealth grows even as employment collapses — citizens save a portion of UBI each month, property appreciates at 4%/yr, and external investments accrue. This is the abundance-era wealth-building story.

06. Pensions policy — promises honoured

What this shows: the AXION transition does not break the social contract. Per MF Pensions Policy v1.0, existing pension promises are honoured in full; pensions are paid in addition to UBI, never clawed back. Three groups, three policies.
Current Retirees (Group 1)
Full existing pension continues unchanged. Receive pension + UBI. Many comfortably better off as deflation lowers basket cost.
Mid-Career (Group 2)
Accrued benefits legally protected. Mandatory new contributions phase down over 7–10 years. Voluntary saving thereafter.
Younger Workers (Group 3)
No mandatory pension contributions. UBI is the primary retirement floor. Voluntary Axion-linked savings if desired.
In this sim: at Y0, ~21% of adults are Group 1 retirees receiving $400/mo pension in addition to UBI. The cohort shrinks at 8.5%/yr (mortality + zero new Group 1 entrants); by Y20 about ~5% of adults remain on full legacy pension. Everyone else (students, full-time caregivers, disabled, workforce-unemployed) receives UBI only — there is no separate safety-net payment.

07. Tax framework — today vs Year 20

What this shows: the AXION tax structure replaces today's tax-heavy system. Income, sales and corporate taxes go to zero. A small Land Value Tax (0.35%, exempt for UBI-eligible homes under $450k) and a flat Colony Bill ($42/mo utilities + $18.50 federal contribution) replace them. Effective tax burden drops from ~18% to ~2.8% — about 85% lower — while UBI-only residents are net-positive every month. Source: Grok V9.13, docs/Grok2.md §2.

08. The argument, summarised

  • AI/robotics displacement is not optional — Grok V9.13 projects national unemployment rising from 4.2% to 75% over 20 years. The conventional (CBO) view ignores this. AXION confronts it.
  • UBI scales with productivity, not with the labour market. MAC charges on corporate profits grow as AI productivity compounds at 5.4%/yr, so UBI rises from $137 to $394/mo per adult — automatic, not budget-vote dependent.
  • Consumption is sustained through the transition. Even as employment crashes, total monthly consumption holds up — UBI replaces wages as the dominant household income source. Mond keeps purchasing power circulating locally.
  • Citizen tax burden drops ~85%. From ~18% effective to ~2.8%. The Colony Bill ($42 utilities + 0.35% LVT + $18.50 federal) replaces income/sales/corporate tax. UBI-only adults are net-positive every month, climbing to +$323/mo by Y20.
  • Living standards improve. UBI grows past Grok's moderate basket for a family of 4 (~$655–950/mo by Y20 vs UBI ≈ $783/mo per family). Deflation in tradables (−33% by Y20) helps further.
  • Wealth compounds. Saved wealth per adult climbs over the 20yr — property equity appreciates, external investments accrue, liquid savings build from the slack in UBI consumption.
  • Promises are kept. Existing pension obligations are honoured in full per MF Pensions Policy v1.0. Pensions paid on top of UBI, never clawed back. Mid-career accrued rights protected.
  • Work becomes optional, not abolished. The 25% who remain employed by Year 20 are doing so by choice or in roles AI cannot fill; everyone else has a stable floor and time to pursue craft, care, or community.
