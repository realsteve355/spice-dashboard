For the purposes of this let's ignore V tokens. S tokens only for a while.

Citizens sell their labour to companies
Companies sell goods and services to the citizens
Citizens pay tax
Companies pay tax


Ai and robotics comes along and automates all labour. There is no longer a market for human labour. All humans are unemployed.
Citizens have no money so cannot buy goods or pay tax
Companies receive no revenue and cannot buy materials or pay tax
Government collapses due to no tax revenues to pay welfare or interest on debt
Economic and societal collapse

Solutions
1) Citizens provide the Ai and robotics rather than human labour (very unlikely - let's discount this)
2) Citizens print the money

Citizens Print The Money

Citizens print S-tokens
External Companies say "we'd like to sell you X for $Y"
Citizens say "We have no $. We are printing a Crypto UBI of S tokens you can have instead. Companies reluctantly agree.
Company goes to supplier to buy input products and says "we'd like to buy some e.g. flour. we can't pay you in $ , because we have run out , but we have these S crypto tokens" Supplier reluctantly agrees.

S-tokens printed are UBI basket + 20%, printed monthly.
Money supply inflation in month 2 is obviously 100%
But every subsequent month this reduces due to dilution.
There is no fractional reserve banking to inflate the money supply via loans.
S tokens are a little like bitcoin , but rather than the bitcoin fixed supply, the Spice supply formula is known (UBI + 20%). Inflation formula is known.
