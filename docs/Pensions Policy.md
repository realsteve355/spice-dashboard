# MaryFontaine Pensions Policy
**Version 1.0**  
**Date:** May 31, 2026  
**Part of the MaryFontaine Abundance Model**

## Core Principle
Honor all existing pension promises while transitioning to a simpler, UBI-centric retirement system as automation and abundance take hold.

## Three Groups of People

### 1. Current Retirees (Already in Payout Mode)
- **Policy**: Full existing pension payments continue unchanged.
- They receive their full pension **on top of** the full Universal Basic Income (UBI).
- **Rationale**: Breaking promises to current retirees would destroy public trust and is politically unacceptable.
- **Outcome**: Many retirees will be financially comfortable or better off due to UBI + deflation lowering living costs.

### 2. Mid-Career Workers (Accrued Rights + Ongoing Contributions)
- **Accrued Benefits**: All pension rights earned before the launch date are legally protected and will be paid out as scheduled when they retire.
- **Future Contributions**: Mandatory new pension contributions are phased down over 7–10 years.
- After the phase-out period, workers can choose voluntary savings (via simplified Axion-linked investment accounts) if they want more than UBI provides.
- **Outcome**: Protected on past service, lower mandatory costs going forward, and UBI becomes the primary floor.

### 3. Younger Workers & New Entrants
- No new mandatory pension contributions after the phase-out period.
- UBI serves as the main retirement security mechanism.
- Individuals are free to save voluntarily in tax-advantaged accounts facilitated by The Fisc and Axion Inc.
- **Outcome**: Much simpler system with lower lifetime costs and greater flexibility.

## Interaction with UBI and Colony Bill
- UBI is **not clawed back** for people receiving pensions.
- Pensions are paid **in addition to** UBI.
- The Colony Company monthly bill (utilities + minimal federal/state contribution) remains the same for everyone.
- High-income pensioners may pay a slightly higher federal/state contribution line item if needed for political fairness, but this is kept minimal.

## Transition Ramp (High-Level)

| Period       | Policy Change |
|--------------|---------------|
| Years 0–3    | Full honoring of all existing pensions + UBI introduction |
| Years 4–10   | Gradual phase-down of new mandatory contributions |
| Years 10–20  | UBI becomes the dominant retirement floor for most residents |

## Funding & Sustainability
- Early years: Higher costs due to legacy pensions while MAC pool is still growing.
- Later years: Costs naturally decline as old defined-benefit schemes mature.
- The closed-loop MAC + UBI system is designed to scale with automation and productivity growth, making the overall system sustainable.

## Philosophical Note
In an abundance economy, the goal shifts from “guaranteeing high replacement income through forced savings” to “providing a solid, dignified floor (UBI) while allowing people freedom to save, invest, or work optionally if they want more.”

This approach balances:
- Legal and moral obligations to current and near-retirees
- Simplicity and freedom for younger generations
- Fiscal sustainability in a world of high automation and optional work
