It's the same economy

Replace every worker with a free robot, but keep paying the same wage bill — now as a basic income. The money flow doesn't change at all.

The point

Today's economy already works: firms pay out incomes, people spend them, that spending is the firms' revenue, and out of it they pay incomes again. Round and round.

Now replace every worker with a perfect, free robot — but have the firm keep paying the same total, as a charge (the MAC) that the Fisc hands straight back out as a basic income. Nothing in the money changes. People get the same income, spend the same, the firms earn and keep the same. The only differences are that the work is now done by robots and the paycheck is called a basic income. It must work — because it is today's economy.
