What this is. The deterministic /ledger and /abundance pages let you scrub one scenario at a time. This page runs four policy regimes side by side through the Mesa ABM scaffold and overlays them. All four use the same colony (Bob/Alice/John/Jane, McDonald's franchise, coffee shop, pottery export) and the same boundary rules — only the UBI policy and citizen savings behaviour differ. The divergence between the lines is what makes the policy choice visible.
