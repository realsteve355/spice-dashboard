Colony v0 — pre-AXION baseline

Open economy under automation pressure. No UBI, no central authority, fixed money supply. Mesa-driven agent simulation, 30 citizens, 3 sectors.
Step 1 — Maryfontaine today. 30 adult citizens (with ~12 children as dependents) in a chain-dominated small town. Each sector has three private providers: a chain branch (Walmart / McDonald's — employs citizens at low wages, ~70% of revenue drains to corporate HQ), a truly-local indy (the independent shop, the artisan, the dentist — revenue stays local, better wages), and pure imports (Amazon, streaming — no local jobs, all revenue drains). Plus a public sector (~23% of workforce — teachers, town-hall, healthcare workers) funded by federal/state transfers. Households with kids get child-credit transfers matched to their consumption boost. The colony's BoP is calibrated to balance: chain corp fees + pure imports flow OUT; exports + federal transfers + child credits flow IN. Next colonies: (2) introduce a local currency, (3) layer automation pressure on top.

What's in the basket

Three consumption categories. Each citizen allocates monthly spending across them by the basket weight. The provider mix (local indy / chain / pure import) varies by sector and shifts as automation makes chain & import cheaper.
Food — 25% of basket
Groceries + meals
  • Groceries (meat, dairy, produce, staples)
  • Fast-food / quick-service restaurants
  • Cafés & coffee shops
  • Sit-down restaurants & takeaway
  • Bakery / specialty food
Provider mix: 60% chain (Walmart, Kroger, McDonald's), 35% local indy (independent grocer, local restaurant), 5% import (Amazon Fresh, meal kits)
Goods — 35% of basket
Physical & durable items
  • Clothing & footwear
  • Electronics (phones, laptops, TVs)
  • Home furniture & furnishings
  • Appliances & tools
  • Auto parts & transport equipment
  • Personal care products, household supplies
  • Books, toys, hobby goods
Provider mix: 65% chain (Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot), 15% local indy (hardware, boutique), 20% import (Amazon online)
Services — 40% of basket
Healthcare, utilities, professional, recreation
  • Healthcare (doctor, dentist, prescriptions)
  • Personal care (haircut, salon, gym)
  • Utilities (electricity, gas, water, internet, phone)
  • Insurance (auto, health, home)
  • Professional (legal, accounting, tax prep)
  • Childcare & education extras
  • Auto repair & maintenance
  • Recreation (cinema, gym, events)
  • Streaming & subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, SaaS)
Provider mix: 30% chain (chain gym, chain banks), 60% local indy (your dentist, accountant, mechanic), 10% import (streaming, online insurance, SaaS)
Note: housing (rent, mortgage interest, property tax) is modelled separately from the basket — those flows go via the Bank/Landlord entity and the public sector, not via the three consumption sectors. Federal/state income tax is also separate. The basket here is the post-tax, post-housing consumption pie.
