Colony v0 — pre-AXION baseline

Open economy under automation pressure. No UBI, no central authority, fixed money supply. Mesa-driven agent simulation, 30 citizens, 3 sectors.
What this is. The pre-AXION baseline, modelling the chain-store reality of a modern small town. Each sector has THREE providers: a chain branch (Walmart / McDonald's / Target — employs citizens locally, but ~70% of revenue drains to corporate HQ outside), a truly-local indy (the independent restaurant, the artisan, the services firm — revenue stays local), and a pure import (Amazon, streaming — no local jobs, all revenue drains). The colony's BoP is balanced at A=0 by export industries — primarily the manufacturing / car factory, which is a truly-local firm selling externally. As automation rises, HQ-driven downsizing hits chain-branch workforce first; chain prices fall; pure-import prices fall further; citizens shift more spending to import; local firms try to pick up slack but the export market also collapses (external automation produces substitutes for what the colony used to export). The colony hollows out into structural unemployment even though monetary collapse may not be catastrophic. AXION's job (in v1) is to interrupt this trajectory.
