Colony v0 — pre-AXION baseline

Open economy under automation pressure. No UBI, no central authority, fixed money supply. Mesa-driven agent simulation, 30 citizens, 3 sectors.
What this is. Step 1 of the colony exploration — today's small town, USD only, no UBI, no automation (default A=0). Each sector has three providers: a chain branch (Walmart / McDonald's / Target — employs citizens locally but ~70% of revenue drains to HQ as corporate fee), a truly-local indy (the independent restaurant, the artisan, the dentist — revenue stays local, premium wages), and pure imports (Amazon, streaming — no local jobs, all revenue drains). Chains employ more workers (more citizens at lower wages) than locals; locals carry the colony's export industries (car factory, etc.) that bring USD back in. The colony's BoP is approximately balanced at A=0 — chain corporate fees + pure imports flowing out, exports flowing in. Crank up A to see what automation does to this picture, but for the first cut, leave A=0 and verify the healthy baseline. Next colonies will explore (a) introducing a local currency on top of this baseline and (b) layering automation on top of both.
