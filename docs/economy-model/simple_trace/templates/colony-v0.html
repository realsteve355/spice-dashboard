Colony v0 — pre-AXION baseline

Open economy under automation pressure. No UBI, no central authority, fixed money supply. Mesa-driven agent simulation, 30 citizens, 3 sectors.
Step 1 — Maryfontaine today. 30 adult citizens (with ~12 children as dependents) in a chain-dominated small town. Each sector has three private providers: a chain branch (Walmart / McDonald's — employs citizens at low wages, ~70% of revenue drains to corporate HQ), a truly-local indy (the independent shop, the artisan, the dentist — revenue stays local, better wages), and pure imports (Amazon, streaming — no local jobs, all revenue drains). Plus a public sector (~23% of workforce — teachers, town-hall, healthcare workers) funded by federal/state transfers. Households with kids get child-credit transfers matched to their consumption boost. The colony's BoP is calibrated to balance: chain corp fees + pure imports flow OUT; exports + federal transfers + child credits flow IN. Next colonies: (2) introduce a local currency, (3) layer automation pressure on top.
