Axion — economy model

The working models behind the Axion methodology.

As AI and robotics remove most paid work, business destroys its own customer base. The fix is to price market participation itself — a Market Access Charge (MAC) levied on firms and paid back to citizens as a basic income. The real dividend of automation is not cheaper goods or larger profits, but free human time.

These pages work through the pieces of that argument. Use the navigation above. This workbench is being rebuilt around the current methodology, so some pages are in transition.
