What the chart shows. Both lines start at 100% in 2026 — same factory, same prices, same headcount. The red line (employment) falls fast as automation displaces workers. The blue line (price) falls slower — the goods get cheaper, but not as fast as the jobs vanish. The shaded gap between the two is the bridge problem: people are unemployed at a faster rate than the basket gets cheap enough for UBI to cover them. That's the years where the Fisc reserve takes the strain.
