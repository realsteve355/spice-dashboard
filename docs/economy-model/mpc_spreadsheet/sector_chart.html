Phase 1 vs Phase 2. Phase 1 ends in 2036 — by then everything currently in-progress has scaled (LLMs in offices, robotaxis everywhere, fully automated warehouses, AI tutors). Phase 2 (2036–2046) is where the things that look impossible today fall: robotic surgeons, humanoid carers, AI-driven construction crews assembling houses, robotic plumbers, robotic farmers. By 2046, basically every job is automatable in principle — the question is just consumer preference for human-delivered service in certain categories (hospitality, care work, personal services hold on partly for this reason).

The big retail line accounts for pass-through deflation: Walmart sells iPhones, and the iPhones get cheaper. So "big retail" deflation is a weighted blend of Walmart's own value-added (slow to deflate) and the products it sells (deflating with their categories).
