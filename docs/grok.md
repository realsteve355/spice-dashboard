\### Land, Housing \& Wealth

\- \*\*Solutions\*\*: Land Value Tax + Colony Company robotic/3D-printed homes.

\- Housing cost: Reduced to 180–280 Mond/month for moderate options.

\- Wealth inequality is less psychologically corrosive; abundance shifts focus to choice and creation.



\### Entrepreneurship \& Incentives

\- \*\*Simplified System\*\* via The Fisc: One-click companies, smart contract shares.

\- \*\*Incentive Evolution\*\*: "Becoming rich" transforms from scarcity-driven necessity to voluntary pursuit of status, impact, creativity, and self-actualization.

\- Automation lowers barriers, making successful entrepreneurship more accessible for those who choose it.



\*\*Governance\*\*: Lean Colony Company monthly billing for automated services. Grok as first-line judge.

\## Overall Model Summary

This framework sustains consumer demand via the Market Access Charge, stabilizes living costs with Mond, addresses housing through automation + LVT, and keeps entrepreneurial dynamism alive even as work becomes optional. Both Islara and MaryFontaine serve as viable pilots for the age of abundance.
