# Axion System: Market Access Charge (MAC), Fisc, and B2B Fisc

## Overview
The Axion system uses a Market Access Charge (MAC) to fund Basic Income in an automated economy. Companies pay for access to customers, replacing the old wage bill. The system starts locally and can scale.

## Core MAC Formula
**MAC per Transaction = Transaction Value × K × (Corporate Profit ÷ Number of Employees)**

- **Transaction Value**: Value of the sale.
- **K**: Dynamic scaling factor.
- **Corporate Profit & Employees**: Company data used for weighting.

## How K is Calculated
K is set quarterly by the Fisc to match required UBI + surplus.

**K = (Required UBI + Target Surplus) ÷ Total Weighted Base**

- Uses previous quarter’s data + forecasts.
- Announced in advance for predictability.

## Fisc (Local Treasury)
- Manages MAC collection and UBI distribution within an Area.
- Holds surplus in USD, bonds, gold, or Bitcoin.
- Operates transparently with public reporting.

## B2B Fisc
- National/regional clearing house for B2B transactions.
- Handles MAC on upstream transactions (law firm to Apple, suppliers, etc.).
- Distributes shares to local Fiscs based on pro-rata rules (e.g., final consumer location or UBI need).

## Tax Treatment
- Customer pays normal price + sales tax. MAC is skimmed from seller.
- MAC is a deductible business expense for companies.
- UBI is taxable income to recipients. Fisc issues 1099 forms.
- Fisc facilitates voluntary tax withholding on UBI unless opted out.
- UBI generally not subject to FICA taxes.

## Enforcement
- "No MAC, No Market Access" — non-compliant companies lose ability to transact in participating Areas via Mond.
- Strong economic incentive for compliance.

## Key Challenges & Mitigations
- **Complexity**: Start with one Fisc + B2B Fisc; keep rules simple.
- **Attribution**: Use pro-rata based on revenue or consumer location; automate via Mond.
- **Avoidance**: Market access denial is the primary lever.
- **Anti-Automation Perception**: Frame MAC as the minimal charge for market stability.
- **Transition Volatility**: Build early surplus for buffer.
- **Equity**: Add nuance (minimums, phase-ins) for small businesses.

## International Supply Chains
- Note for future: Let other countries develop compatible systems (e.g., KorAxion). Focus initial pilots on domestic flows.

This model aims to create a stable bridge from the wage economy to automation abundance while preserving market signals and profits.
