### Taxation Framework (Very Low & Automated)

- Sales Tax: **0%**
- Personal Income Tax (colony): **0%** on UBI and voluntary earnings
- Land Value Tax: **0.35%** on unimproved land (full exemption for UBI-only primary homes < $450k)
- Corporate Income Tax (colony): **0%**
- Federal/State Contribution: Small fixed line item on Colony Bill (~$18.50/adult/month)
- Overall Colony Burden: **2.5–4.5%**

**Fisc Automatic Rules (Code-Ready)**:
- UBI credited first every month
- Automatic LVT check + exemption for qualifying residents
- Fixed utilities + federal/state contribution added
- Net bill/credit issued in Mond
- All flows transparent on blockchain

### Mond Payment System
- Context-aware: Local payments settle in Mond, external auto-converts to USD (zero fee/spread for residents).
- Starts as USD-backed stablecoin, with pathway to hybrid basket (USD + Bitcoin + Gold).
- UBI paid exclusively in Mond for local stability.

### Axion Inc.
- For-profit commercial company earning 0.15% gas fee on Mond transactions.
- Revenue funds operations and growth.

### Land, Housing & Wealth in Abundance
- Robotic/3D-printed homes widely available within UBI.
- Modest LVT with exemptions prevents forced sales.
- Wealth inequality exists but is less psychologically corrosive; entrepreneurship remains open via simplified Fisc company formation.

**Incentive Evolution**: The drive to "become rich" shifts from scarcity survival to status, creativity, impact, and voluntary achievement.
